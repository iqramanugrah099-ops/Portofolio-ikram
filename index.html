About me

Capturing stories through a lens

I'm M. Iqram Anugrah, an English Literature student at Universitas Halu Oleo based in Kendari. What started as a hobby of photographing everyday moments has grown into a passion for visual storytelling — from quiet portraits to cinematic short videos.

I love finding beauty in ordinary places: warm light after rain, the rhythm of the city, and the people who live in it. Every frame is a small story waiting to be told.
